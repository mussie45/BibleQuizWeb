📖 About BibleQuiz

BibleQuiz is designed to help believers, students, and curious minds discover the richness of Scripture through interactive questions and verse references.

Questions are sourced directly from canonical Scripture accounts and the King James Version (KJV) dataset containing 31,102 verses.
